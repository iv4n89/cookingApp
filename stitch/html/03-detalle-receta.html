Preparation

01

Prepare the Base

Finely mince the shallots and sauté in a heavy-bottomed pan with a splash of olive oil until translucent and fragrant.

02

Toast the Grains

Add the Arborio rice to the pan. Toast for 2 minutes, stirring constantly until the edges are translucent but the center remains white.

03

Slow Integration

Begin adding warm vegetable stock one ladle at a time. Wait for the liquid to be almost entirely absorbed before adding the next.

04

Mushroom Infusion

Halfway through the process, fold in the sautéed wild mushrooms and fresh thyme leaves to allow the flavors to meld.
